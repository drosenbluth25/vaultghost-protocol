# VaultGhost Operational / Research Boundary

## Operational Core

Claims in the operational core must be tied to executable behavior, defined schemas, cryptographic checks, deterministic transitions, fixtures, and explicit limitations.

Operational subjects may include:

- artifact hashing and integrity checks;
- canonical serialization;
- signature creation and verification;
- manifest and receipt validation;
- evidence-state classification;
- contradiction preservation;
- policy-gated dispositions;
- auditable limitation reporting.

## Experimental Research

The following subjects remain research hypotheses unless supported by a separately reviewed empirical method:

- latent replication across proprietary LLM systems;
- hidden prompt-influence attribution;
- EchoShell or displacement-buffer effects;
- Drift Anchor replication-decay measurements;
- latent covariance or contamination registries;
- causal attribution without internal model telemetry.

## Non-Inheritance Rule

A research hypothesis must not inherit the assurance level of a cryptographically verified artifact. Hash validity, signature validity, timestamp validity, or deterministic replay do not establish that a latent-influence hypothesis is true.

## Publication Rule

Canonical operational specifications should describe only implemented and testable guarantees. Experimental concepts must be labelled `EXPERIMENTAL_RESEARCH`, include methodology and falsification criteria, and remain outside conformance certification until independently replicated.
